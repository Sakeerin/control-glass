# Contract-Driven Architecture Arena (`/arena`)

Compare 2-3 competing TypeScript contract designs (`Brand<T>`, `Result<T, E>`) and score them on Level 1 Compile-Time Impossibility (40pts), Boundary Isolation (30pts), Zero-Indirection Simplicity (20pts), and Empirical Testability (10pts).
